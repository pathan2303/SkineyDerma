Our story

Where clinical skill meets quiet luxury

SkinNey was created around a simple idea: skin and aesthetic care should feel as reassuring as it is effective. Our clinic sits above Starbucks on Chandan Shanti Link Road, in Mira-Bhayandar, a tranquil step away from the city's pace.

Here, advanced treatments such as PRP, GFC, laser, MNRF and HIFU are paired with artistry in microblading and lip blush, always guided by a proper consultation and clear, realistic expectations.
